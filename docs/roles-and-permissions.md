# SENTINEL roles and permissions

## School Community

Students, teachers, staff, parents, and other public users. They can respond to surveys, submit incident or hazard reports, receive approved announcements/alerts, and track only their own report reference.

## Designated Personnel

DRRM coordinators and personnel appointed by the school leader. They can create surveys, access authorized analytics, triage and update assigned reports, and draft announcements or alerts. Their communications require school-leader approval before publication.

## School Leader

The principal or an authorized school leader. This role has full operational authority: approvals, publication, reports, analytics, assignments, users, roles, and audit history.

## ICT / System Administrator

Technical administrators. They manage accounts, account status, and the location hierarchy (campuses, buildings, floors, rooms, and areas). They do not automatically have access to confidential report contents or anonymous reporter data.

## Role-assignment rule

Only a School Leader can assign or revoke roles. System Administrators may manage accounts but cannot elevate access.
